# BidSpace product direction

## Diagnosis

BidSpace is a temporary vendor-space marketplace connecting hosts who control
short-term commercial space with vendors who want to occupy it. The repository
already aligns on its strongest foundations: host and vendor organizations,
reusable venue/space inventory, spatial discovery, sealed bids, curated host
selection, invitations, messaging, documents, and trust provenance.

The product had drifted in three important ways:

1. Internal implementation language ("inventory units" and "opportunities")
   was more prominent than the customer language (spaces and placements).
2. Payment, payout, and production-activation copy implied a live marketplace.
   BidSpace is not live and payments must remain dormant until a founder gate.
3. Bids were the only persisted vendor submission even though future supply may
   be allocated by application, invite, fixed fee, approval, or waitlist.

## Preserve

- Map-first discovery and venue/site-plan precision.
- Hosts, vendors, organizations, venues, events, and reusable space records.
- Sealed bids with host-curated selection; highest bid never auto-wins.
- Private vendor networks, invitations, contextual messaging, and saved spaces.
- Verification provenance, requirements, documents, and explainable fit.

## Replace or correct

- Lead with space, vendor, host, and placement in customer-facing copy.
- Treat opportunity/inventory-unit as implementation terms, not the product pitch.
- Replace payment promises with an explicit pre-launch, no-money state.
- Separate applications from bids while keeping a unified placement-review view.
- Replace payout configuration with a dormant future fee-plan explanation.

## MVP surfaces

The next MVP should prove a complete non-monetary loop: a host creates a space,
describes dates/traffic/requirements, discovers or invites vendors, a vendor
bids or applies with a credible profile, and the host compares, messages,
shortlists, approves, declines, or waitlists that submission. Payment and legal
commitment begin only after separate founder approval.
